# Brief

window-navigation-and-scroll (bugfix, user-reported, goes before search-and-substitute). The user reports: after a split they cannot move the cursor/focus to the other window; they cannot move focus from or to the explorer window; PageUp and PageDown do nothing. Facts found: config/default.caratrc binds <C-w>h/j/k/l/w/W/p only in keymap.normal; the explorer tree has its own keys (docs/actions.md plugin.explorer.*), apparently with no <C-w> window moves; Key::PAGE_UP and PAGE_DOWN are decoded (carat/adapters/key_decoder.rb, carat/domain/key_notation.rb) but nothing is bound and no scroll actions exist (no <C-f>, <C-b>, <C-d>, <C-u>, <C-e>, <C-y>). Ask: first reproduce the focus bug through the PTY smoke test and in table tests, find the root cause (for example the <C-w> prefix decoding, the pending-key timeout, or the focus use case) and fix it; make <C-w> h/j/k/l/w/W/p move focus both ways between split windows and the explorer window; add scroll actions with Neovim semantics: <PageUp>/<PageDown> and <C-b>/<C-f> by a page, <C-u>/<C-d> by half a page with the scroll option, <C-e>/<C-y> by a line keeping the cursor in view, and zz/zt/zb if small; bind them in every relevant mode map through config/default.caratrc and make gen-config, list them in docs/actions.md and docs/compatibility.md with table-driven tests. Keep allocations per key at the baseline. Security review is never skipped.
