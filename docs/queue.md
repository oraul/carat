## Decisions

- `agent-inputs` is delivered now (T1, goal 1, the main session's decision): runs take 1.0M to 1.6M total tokens against 0.24M to 0.40M output, so what agents read dominates; `buffer-global` follows, meeting the every-three rule.
- Size check, `agent-inputs` estimated medium (under 1,200,000 total tokens) with four parts: `pipeline-speed-c` took 1,014,694 in 27.3 minutes, `pipeline-speed-b` 1,476,371 in 62.0 (four parts, two folds, over). The brief's fold, proposal 1 of `pipeline-speed-c` (72.5% of output tokens on a resume), would be a fifth part, so it leads `run-resume` (T1).
- `pipeline-durability` held seven fixes (`guard-allowlist`, seven, took 5 rework rounds) and keeps four; the resume and status fixes join `run-resume` with advisory notes 2 to 5 of `pipeline-speed-c`; the merger's sleep joins `run-budgets` with proposal 2 (22 to 24 merge calls in seven runs).
- `check-skip` (T1) joins proposal 3 (142 of 246 stage seconds), the periodic dev-build key finding, and advisory note 1, one function. T1 order: user requests, the Medium (6), quality first pass 6/15, then items 6 and 7, smaller savings (one resume; 142 s of 27.3 minutes).
- Periodic review at f31f4e8 (security low=2, quality advisory=7): the Low (6) in `substitute-tidy`, moved ahead of `search-tidy` by value (lost buffers against a 1.5 s stall) and, at nine fixes, split into `substitute-structure`; the Low (3) in `guard-lows`; the delivered list and moved citations in `docs-currency`; two stale backlog figures are this change's bookkeeping; advisory note 6 in `guard-advisories`.
- Security first, `security_first` empty: `pipeline-speed-c`'s review at e8c5b43 high=0 medium=0 low=0, the periodic review at f31f4e8 high=0 medium=0 low=2, and the Medium (6) at e20c83c, below 8, is item 4. Security review is never skipped.
- Every-three rule: items 2 to 9 (T1 and T3) are eight changes without a feature, allowed since T0 to T3 keep their place; items 10, 14, and 18 (bars, split from the UI track) then keep one UI change in every three.
- Aging, one push-back per plan moving an item down: items 8 to 26 but 15, 18, and 19. Written reason at three or more: T1 (goal 1, the user's order) and T3 items keep their place ahead of T4 to T6, and items 21 to 25 depend on the UI track.
- Queue size: under the 17,369 bytes `agent-inputs` sets (23,716 at f31f4e8), no finding dropped.
- `pipeline-speed-c`'s row (pull request 51, rework 0, every gate first pass, 235,876 output and 1,014,694 total tokens) is appended by this change's package step; quality first pass becomes 6/15.
- Standing user decisions: frontmatter may name a tier alias, the cheaper for mechanical phases; the bars live in the core; the leader change is breaking; the UI track follows Neovim command support, and the two plugins follow it; no dashboard.
