## Decisions

- `buffer-global` now (T5, goal 7): after feature #50, #51 and #52 were tooling, so the every-three rule needs a feature; no T0 open.
- Size: `buffer-global` medium (under 1,200,000 total): one command family reusing `buffer-substitute`'s ranges and search (1,642,481 in 114.4 min); `agent-inputs` took 923,419 in 33.6.
- Security first empty: `agent-inputs` 0/0/0; periodic f31f4e8 low=2; Medium (6) at e20c83c, below 8, is item 3.
- `phase-shares` (T1) places `agent-inputs` retro notes 5 and 6 (plan 36.9%, scout 20.3% of output tokens), ahead of item 6 (one resume) since it recurs every run; it folds advisories 1 to 3, advisory 4 is fixed above.
- Every-three: items 1 to 9 are T1 and T3 and keep their place; a UI change then at 10, 13, 16, 19, and 22 (the leader layout split out), never three non-features in a row.
- Aging: +1 for each item behind `phase-shares` not moved up; at three or more T1 and T3 keep their place, items 23 to 26 follow the UI track.
