Every numbered item carries its tier (`T0` to `T6`) and its size (`estimated small`, `estimated medium`, or `estimated large`), and states the problem, its numbers, and the acceptance check rather than the implementation.

## Decisions

- `pipeline-speed-c` is delivered now and leaves the queue (T1 user decision, goal 1): the main session placed it ahead of `buffer-global` because T1 ranks above T5 and the every-three rule was just met by `buffer-substitute` (pull request 50); `buffer-global` follows as item 1, pushed back 1.
- Size check, `pipeline-speed-c` estimated medium (under 1,200,000 total tokens), delivered as its OpenSpec change holds it: parts (a) and (b), the three folds of the old item 2 (`check` on a dirty tree, its own `phase.check`, `add_note`'s two patterns), and the minute dash of `budget_line`, taken from `pipeline-durability` because part (b) rewrites that line. The comparable rows are tooling changes of the same scripts: `pipeline-speed` 1,212,191 total tokens in 56.5 minutes and `pipeline-speed-b`, four parts and two folds, 1,476,371 in 62.0 minutes, over its medium budget (overrun note 13); the branch at fa2c163 edits 16 files, 446 lines added and 163 removed. The brief's three candidates would make eight fixes, past the rule of about four, so none is folded in.
- The tool-call cap and the early budget warning of the user's insights review (T1 user request) become `run-budgets`, item 3, rather than joining `agent-inputs`, which already holds four parts: both read the run's size estimate against its spend (contributor 154 calls in `guard-allowlist`, 105 in `buffer-substitute`). It follows `agent-inputs` and builds on the `under:` and `over:` budget line this change writes.
- The superseded or killed check of the `buffer-substitute` retrospective (note 11, T1) goes into `pipeline-durability`, not `agent-inputs` as the brief offered: it is the same event as that item's killed check reported at stage none, and it cost rework round 2 of 3, which made no commit. The minute-dash fold leaves that item, fixed here.
- Retrospective notes of `buffer-substitute`, each placed (T1): note 10 (`has_` on record members, its one blocking finding, quality first pass 0 for the seventh run, 5 of 14) in `implement-contracts`; note 12 (scout 93,883 output tokens and 93 calls, 24.0%) in `agent-inputs` with note 7 of `pipeline-speed-b`; note 13 (`pipeline-speed-b` over at 1,476,371 against 1,200,000) is the evidence of the size check above.
- Quality advisories of `buffer-substitute`, notes 2 to 9, six entries in `docs/backlog.md` from one domain (T6): the rubric queues a domain alone at five, so they become `substitute-tidy`, item 16, ahead of `guard-advisories` by value: two are user-visible wrong answers (`n` after `:s/A/x/i`, `:&` after a refused `:s/a/b/c`). Its blocking note 1 was fixed before the merge.
- Security first, `security_first` empty: the periodic security review at e20c83c reports `high=0 medium=1 low=1`, its Medium at confidence 6, below the bar of 8, placed in `merge-gate-integrity`; the delivery review of `buffer-substitute` at 127a14f reports `high=0 medium=0 low=0 blocking=0`. No periodic review is due: 1 of 5 changes merged since e20c83c and no sensitive area touched (`review-due buffer-substitute`). Security review is never skipped.
- The three ideas of the user's insights review stay in `docs/backlog.md`, where this change records them, folded under item 23: classifying CI failures (CI first pass 17 of 17, no fix round to save), parallel delivery in worktrees (saves wall time, not tokens, and nearly every feature edits `config/default.caratrc`, `docs/actions.md`, `docs/compatibility.md`, the code map, and the queue), and a replay benchmark (about 1M total tokens per replay, near a medium budget each).
- Every-three rule: `buffer-global` is the feature of the next three changes; items 2 to 8 are then seven changes with no user-requested feature, because six T1 items (goal 1, the user's decision) and two T3 items keep their place ahead of a feature; theme tokens follows them, and items 13 and 18 keep one UI change after every three non-feature changes.
- Aging, counted by position against `docs/queue.md` at 206f1b7: the delivered `pipeline-speed-c` and the new `run-budgets` leave items 2 to 15 at their numbers, so none of them is pushed back; `substitute-tidy` moves items 17 to 23 down one; those at three or more carry a written reason in their line.
- Queue size (T1, `agent-inputs`): two new items, `run-budgets` and `substitute-tidy`, take `docs/queue.md` from 21,599 to about 23,500 bytes, further from the 17,369 budget that `agent-inputs` sets; that item, item 2, carries the budget rather than this plan dropping placed findings.
