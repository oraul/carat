Every numbered item carries its tier (`T0` to `T6`) and its size (`estimated small`, `estimated medium`, or `estimated large`), and states the problem, its numbers, and the acceptance check rather than the implementation.

## Decisions

- `buffer-substitute` is delivered now and leaves the queue (T5 user-approved roadmap feature, roadmap item 6, goal 7): the every-three rule is due, since `guard-allowlist` (pull request 47), `pipeline-speed` (48), and `pipeline-speed-b` (49) are three non-feature changes after `buffer-search` (46); it was pushed back 3.
- Size check, split as the brief allows: the whole item is four separate parts (ranges, `:s` with its flags and replacement characters, the three repeat forms, and `:g` with `:g!` and `:v`), and `'<,'>` needs the last visual selection's lines, which no file under `carat/domain/` keeps. The comparable row, `buffer-search` (large, 239,005 output tokens, 1 rework, quality 102,654), is about 1.15M total at the measured ratio of 4.77 to 4.90 total per output token (`pipeline-speed-b` 1,476,371 over 309,581, `pipeline-speed` 1,212,191 over 247,172); the whole item at 1.5 to 2 times that reaches the 2.5M large budget. `buffer-substitute` delivers the ranges with `:s`, `:&`, `&`, and `g&`, estimated large (1.2M to 1.8M total); `buffer-global` (`:g`, `:g!`, `:v`) is queued as item 1, estimated medium, placed right after it by the brief's order.
- Security first, `security_first` empty: the periodic security review at e20c83c reports `high=0 medium=1 low=1`, its Medium at confidence 6 (`.claude/workflows/deliver-change.js:292`), below the bar of 8, placed in `merge-gate-integrity`; the delivery review of `pipeline-speed-b` at 5376d5029ce4 reports `high=0 medium=0 low=0 blocking=0`. Security review is never skipped.
- `pipeline-speed-c` is split three ways by the size check (T1): `pipeline-speed-b`, four parts and two folds, went over its medium budget at 1,476,371 total tokens against 1,200,000 in 62.0 minutes (overrun note 8), and `pipeline-speed-c` held four parts and five folds. Parts (a) and (b) keep the name; part (c) joins retrospective notes 6 and 7 as `agent-inputs`; part (d) and its folds become `pipeline-durability`. Each is two or three parts, estimated medium.
- Retrospective notes of `pipeline-speed-b`, each placed (T1): note 6 (plan 33,425 output tokens, queue 28,136 bytes against 17,369 at pull request 42) and note 7 (scout 30,144 output tokens and 24 calls, implement still 62 calls) in `agent-inputs`. This plan already writes a shorter queue toward note 6's budget.
- Periodic review at e20c83c, each finding placed: security Medium (6) and the quality findings at `.claude/workflows/deliver-change.js:292` and `tools/tools_test.sh:756` in the new T1 item `merge-gate-integrity`, with the stages-gate advisory of `pipeline-speed-b` (`tools/gates.sh:195`), the same merge-gates function; security Low (4) `tools/run_state.sh:309` in `pipeline-speed-c` with the `phase.check` entry of the same `check_stages`; quality `.claude/workflows/deliver-change.js:312` in `implement-contracts` with the fix-review scope advisory (`:375`), the same review loop whose cost that item measures (quality 22.8% of output tokens). The `add_note` advisory (`tools/run_state.sh:577`) is folded into part (b) of `pipeline-speed-c`, which rewrites the closing records.
- Every-three rule: after `buffer-substitute` and `buffer-global`, items 2 to 8 are seven changes in a row with no user-requested feature, because five T1 items (goal 1, the user's decision) and two T3 items keep their place ahead of a feature; theme tokens follows them, and items 13 and 17 keep one UI change after every three non-feature changes.
- Aging: the inserted items move `implement-contracts` and items 7 to 22 down, each counted as one push-back; those at three or more carry a written reason in their line (`ci-hardening` and `project-config-guards` 3, `docs-currency` 3, `file-access-tidy` 6, the UI track's rest, `markdown-preview`, `csv-viewer`, `indent-guides`, language services, and the backlog item 4).
